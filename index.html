🟢 Bullish Engulfing High Reliability (84%)
A small red candle completely engulfed by a large green candle after a downtrend. Confirms buyer dominance and supply exhaustion.
Confirmation: Next candle opens/closes above engulfing close
Optimal Stop: Below engulfing swing low
🟢 Morning Star High Reliability (88%)
Three-candle bullish reversal: long red bar, gap-down indecision star/doji, and strong green bar closing well into the first candle's body.
Confirmation: 3rd candle closes > 50% into 1st candle
Optimal Stop: 1.5 ATR below star shadow
🔴 Evening Star High Reliability (86%)
Three-candle bearish reversal at market peaks. Long green candle, gap-up indecision candle, followed by strong red candle closing into the first body.
Confirmation: 3rd candle volume surge confirms liquidation
Optimal Stop: Above star high
